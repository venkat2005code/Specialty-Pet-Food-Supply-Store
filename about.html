We're on a Mission to Keep Pets Happy & Healthy

Founded by pet lovers, built for pet parents — PawStore is India's most trusted specialty pet store.

From a Small Pet Shop to India's Favourite Pet Store

PawStore started in 2018 in a small shop in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Our founder, a lifelong dog lover, struggled to find premium, vet-approved food for his Labrador, Bruno. That frustration became the spark that started it all.

What began as a single storefront has grown into an online platform serving 12,000+ pet parents across India. Every product we carry is hand-selected by our in-house veterinary team to ensure safety, nutrition, and quality.

Today, PawStore is more than a store — it's a community of people who believe pets deserve the very best. We're proud to be part of your pet's journey.
