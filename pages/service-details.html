Frequently Asked Questions

What age is this food suitable for?

This formula is designed for adult dogs aged 1-7 years. For puppies or senior dogs, please check our specialized formulas.

How much should I feed my dog?

Feeding guidelines vary by weight and activity level. Generally, feed 1-2 cups per 25 lbs of body weight daily, divided into two meals.

Is this suitable for dogs with allergies?

Yes! This grain-free formula is ideal for dogs with food sensitivities. It contains no corn, wheat, soy, or artificial additives.
