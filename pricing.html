Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PawStore membership

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your membership at any time from your dashboard. There are no cancellation fees and your access continues until the end of your billing period.

What is included in the monthly subscription box?

Each box is curated by our vet team and includes 4-6 items such as treats, toys, grooming samples, and health supplements tailored to your pet type.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

Is the 7-day free trial available on all plans?

Yes, all three plans come with a 7-day free trial. No credit card is required to start your trial.
